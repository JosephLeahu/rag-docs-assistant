# Cascades Volcano Observatory (CVO)

## Overview
The USGS Cascades Volcano Observatory is located in Vancouver, Washington. It was established in 1980
in response to the eruption of Mount St. Helens. CVO monitors and studies volcanoes in Washington,
Oregon, and Idaho, including Mount Baker, Glacier Peak, Mount Rainier, Mount St. Helens, Mount Adams,
Mount Hood, Mount Jefferson, Three Sisters, Newberry, and Crater Lake.

## The national program
CVO is one of five USGS volcano observatories in the Volcano Hazards Program. The others are the
Alaska Volcano Observatory (AVO), the California Volcano Observatory (CalVO), the Hawaiian Volcano
Observatory (HVO), and the Yellowstone Volcano Observatory (YVO).

## Partners
CVO works closely with the Pacific Northwest Seismic Network (PNSN), headquartered at the University of
Washington, which operates many of the seismometers used to watch Cascade volcanoes. CVO also partners with
state emergency management agencies, county governments, and the National Weather Service.

## Mission
CVO's work combines real-time monitoring, geologic hazard assessments, and public outreach so that
communities and emergency managers can prepare for and respond to volcanic unrest.
